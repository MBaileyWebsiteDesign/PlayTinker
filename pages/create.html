Pip asks before it answers

Instead of making the whole game for you, Pip asks "What should happen when…?" and "What do you think this will do?" Thinking first makes you a better creator.

Small steps you understand

AI adds one feature at a time and explains it. Every AI-written line is highlighted, so you always know which parts you need to understand.

Everything is saved as versions

Every save is a version you can go back to. Break something? Roll back. That's how real developers work.

The Prompt Builder

A prompt is the instruction you give an AI. Write one below and the checker will tell you what's good, what's missing and why. Every check shows its reasoning, so a grown-up can review it if needed.

Don't include your name, school or address. Nothing here is sent to an AI in this demo.

Five ingredients of a great prompt

  1. Goal: what you want done
  2. Context: what you're making and what already works
  3. Details and rules: numbers, keys, what happens if…
  4. Example: "like a trampoline", "like snake"
  5. Ask to understand: "explain each part to me"
