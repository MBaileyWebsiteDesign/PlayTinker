Quality over "AI slop"

The Creator's Check

Before a project counts as finished, it's checked against five principles. A game that looks great but doesn't work won't earn a creator badge.

1IntentCan you explain what you wanted to build?
2OwnershipCan you explain the parts AI helped with?
3FunctionalityDoes it actually work?
4QualityTested on different devices and situations?
5ImprovementBetter after testing or feedback?

How the check works

AI does the checking, and always shows its working. For every principle it records a verdict, its justification, the evidence it used and how confident it is. If it's unsure, borderline, or spots something like personal information, it flags the check for a human review.

Parents, teachers and moderators can read the full record and overrule any decision. Nothing is a black box.

What gets recorded

  • The verdict for each principle
  • The justification: why it decided that
  • The evidence it relied on
  • A confidence level
  • Whether a person should review it, and why
  • Which checker and version made the decision

This demo uses simple rules so it runs without any AI. In the real platform, Claude does the check and returns exactly the same kind of record.
