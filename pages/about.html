About

Why PlayTinker exists

To help people, especially children, move from consuming technology to confidently creating it, and to use AI thoughtfully rather than blindly.

PlayTinker brings together three things: playing great games, creating your own with AI as a helper, and mastering the real skills behind software: planning, prompting, debugging, testing and releasing.

The child is the creator. AI is the tool. Learning is the product.
