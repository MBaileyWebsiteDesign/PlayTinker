Parents & Educators

Helping children become creators, not just consumers

PlayTinker is free for everyone, with no adverts. It's designed with children in mind, but open to learners of any age, including the grown-ups helping them.

Why it matters

AI literacy

Children will use AI all their lives. Knowing how to explain clearly, check answers and take responsibility is a skill worth learning early.

Learning by making

Games give instant, honest feedback: they work or they don't. Making one brings in logic, maths, creativity, testing and empathy for the player.

Quality, not "slop"

We reward understanding and improvement, never the number of prompts. The first AI answer is rarely the finished product.

You can change your mind

Withdraw a public game at any time from the parent dashboard.

No personal details shown

Public games show a creator nickname only: no real names, photos, schools or locations.

Reviewed and reportable

Public games are moderated, and anyone can report a problem.

AI checks, with the working shown

When a creator finishes a project, AI runs the Creator's Check. It doesn't just say pass or fail. It records why: its justification for each principle, the evidence it used, how confident it is, and whether a person should review it.

You or a teacher can read that record and overrule it. If the AI is unsure, borderline, or spots personal information, it flags the check for human review automatically.

Your role

Set up and control

You create and manage your child's account, AI limits and sharing.

Be a play-tester

Playing their game and giving honest feedback is part of the learning.

Ask good questions

"How does that work?" "What would you change?" You don't need to know any code.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. PlayTinker is planned to be completely free, with no adverts and no selling of data.

What age is it for?

There's no minimum or maximum age. It's designed with children in mind, especially to build prompting skills, but anyone can use it. Younger children should use it with an adult.

Which AI does it use?

Inside the platform, Claude (made by Anthropic) will power Pip and the Creator's Check, through our own server, so no AI keys are ever in the browser. Our team uses ChatGPT to create artwork. We'll always tell you how prompts are handled.

Can children chat with each other?

No. There is no public child-to-child messaging.

Is it launched?

Not yet. This is a concept preview. We're looking for families, teachers and schools to help shape it.
