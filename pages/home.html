The Academy

The big idea

Better prompts. Better thinking. Better creations.

Anyone can ask an AI to "make a game". The result is usually generic and the person learns nothing. PlayTinker teaches the skills that make AI genuinely useful: explaining clearly, giving context, checking answers, testing and improving.

It's designed with children in mind, but it's open to everyone: parents, teachers, grandparents, anyone curious.
