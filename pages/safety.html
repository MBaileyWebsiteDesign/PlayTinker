Privacy & Safety

Safety is a founding principle

These are the commitments we're designing PlayTinker around. This is a concept: formal assessments and specialist advice will come before launch.

We will

  • Keep PlayTinker free, with no adverts and no behavioural tracking
  • Collect only what's genuinely needed
  • Never sell anyone's data
  • Keep projects private by default
  • Only make games public with parent or guardian permission
  • Explain clearly how AI is used, in child-friendly language
  • Keep AI keys on our servers, never in the browser
  • Let parents export or delete data at any time

We won't

  • Allow public child-to-child messaging
  • Track location
  • Use third-party advertising or tracking scripts (this site has none)
  • Show real names, photos or schools on public games
  • Claim we collect "no data" when an AI service processes prompts
  • Claim legal compliance before proper assessments are complete

How AI is used, honestly

Claude, for creating and checking

When a creator asks Pip for help or runs the Creator's Check, the request goes to our server, which removes personal details and then asks Claude. Anthropic processes that request to produce the answer.

ChatGPT, for our artwork

The PlayTinker team uses ChatGPT to create images such as Pip and game scenes. Creators' personal details are never sent there by us.

Checks you can review

Every AI check records its verdict, justification, evidence and confidence, and flags anything uncertain for a person to review.

Games run in a sandbox

Every game, including ones built with AI, runs inside a locked "sandbox". It can't read your account, cookies or other pages, and it can't reach the rest of the website. Even if a game had a mistake in it, it couldn't touch anything personal.

This website already works this way: open any game in the library and it runs in a sandboxed frame.

Designed with the rules in mind

We are designing with UK GDPR and the ICO's Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code) in mind: data minimisation, privacy by default and clear explanations. Before launch we will complete a Data Protection Impact Assessment and take specialist legal and safeguarding advice, including on consent, AI provider terms and moderation.

This concept website collects no personal data, sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The demo checkers run on our server using simple rules and don't store what you type.
